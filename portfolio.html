01 / Who I Am

About Me

I'm a software engineer with a background in electrical engineering and a habit of building things that solve real problems. I like working on both backend systems and frontend interfaces, as long as the outcome is clean, predictable, and useful in the real world.

A lot of my work sits at the intersection of public systems, healthcare tech, and Web3. I've worked on patient record systems with offline sync, document workflows with e-signatures, and agentic AI tools that plug into existing workflows instead of replacing them. I enjoy taking vague requirements, understanding the real need, and then shaping them into straightforward, maintainable software.

Outside of code, I like calisthenics, music, and low-budget trips to the hills. I also spend time in developer communities, sharing what I learn, learning from others, and contributing to open-source tools when I can. I strongly believe small, honest contributions and collaboration move technology forward more than buzzwords ever will.
